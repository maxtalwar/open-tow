# Roadmap

## 0.2 — Research instrumentation

- Scenario parameter overrides and batch-experiment manifests
- CSV/JSON after-action export
- Unit and metric time-series charts
- State hashing and replay import/export
- Property-based invariant tests

## 0.3 — Better uncertainty and information

- Probabilistic contact tracks rather than exact enemy locations
- Detection, identification, deception, and command-delay mechanics
- Separate Taiwan ground map and maneuver graph
- Airbase parking, shelter, runway, repair, and sortie-generation submodels
- Explicit tanker and logistics nodes

## 0.4 — Scenario excursions

- Japanese participation and basing variants
- Mainland-strike restrictions
- Taiwanese readiness and warning variants
- Munitions and airbase-hardening variants
- Blockade and coercion scenario family

## 0.5 — Agent research

- PettingZoo parallel environment
- Masked hierarchical action space
- LLM adapter and structured reasoning traces
- Human-versus-agent and agent-league tournament runner
- Behavioral evaluation on held-out scenario excursions

## Validation track

- Obtain and document permissions guidance from CSIS
- Request full public/unclassified umpire and order-of-battle materials
- Conduct structured playtests with professional wargamers
- Publish a model card and sensitivity report for each tagged release
