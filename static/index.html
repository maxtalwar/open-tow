What this is—and is not

This independent educational reconstruction implements the public CSIS structure: 3.5-day turns, approximately 600 km operational areas, a separate 30 km Taiwan ground layer, mission packages, weapon-specific magazines, ASW contact development, and amphibious sustainment.

Counter quantities, probabilities, inventories, topology, doctrine agents, and victory thresholds remain transparent synthetic reconstructions because CSIS did not publish its complete umpire rules or order of battle. This is not official software or a forecast.
