About this model

An independent, open-source digitization of the structure described in the CSIS Taiwan Operational Wargame. It is educational software, not a forecast, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CSIS or any government.

Implemented from the public report

3.5-day turns; roughly 600 km operational areas; a separate 30 km Taiwan ground layer; mission packages; weapon-specific magazines; ASW contact development; amphibious lift and sustainment.

Reconstructed

Counter quantities, probabilities, inventories, area topology, doctrine agents, and victory thresholds are transparent synthetic parameters, because CSIS did not publish its full umpire rules or order of battle.

Map data

Coastlines and boundaries from Natural Earth (public domain). Elevation and bathymetry from AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1). Operational-area boundaries are schematic.
